## Group D: a program of its own

v0.2.3 (nine items) needs a hand-written backward pass for the LLaMA block, then distillation,
LoRA recovery, exit heads and cost accounting, each measured on a small model. On a CPU this is
weeks, not hours. It is split in the BACKLOG (THME-3036 and the v0.2.3 tickets) and done last.
The first slice is the backward pass checked against finite differences on a tiny model.

## Decisions of 27 November 2026 on the training program

- The in-Rust CPU backward pass and LoRA are merged and checked against Hugging Face to about 1e-12 on a
  tiny model; they stay the reference.
- The PyTorch and PEFT bridge for training real models on the GPU is **later**, not now. The PyTorch
  environment stays only as the independent oracle.
- A CUDA backward pass inside THME is **shelved to a far-future milestone** (v0.5 in the BACKLOG, THME-3107).
  The verified CPU backward pass and the PyTorch oracle are what such a kernel would be tested against.
- Consequence for v0.2.3: items that need training a real model (quantization-aware distillation,
  recovery on quantized weights, exit heads, cost accounting, recovered-candidate reports) stay open until
  the bridge exists. They are not marked done on the strength of the tiny-model tests.
